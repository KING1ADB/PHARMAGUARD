# PHARMAGUARD AI

## Competition Technical Demonstration Plan

### Proving A Real Autonomous Pharmacy Intelligence Agent

## 1. Demonstration Objective

The goal of the demo:

Demonstrate PharmaGuard AI operating as a digital pharmacy employee that monitors operations, detects problems, explains decisions, and initiates actions.

## 2. Demo Scenario

### Scenario:

A community pharmacy in Douala is connected to PharmaGuard AI.

The pharmacy has:

- 5,000 medicine records

- inventory data

- sales history

- supplier information

The AI agent has been running for 30 days.

## 3. Demo Flow Overview

